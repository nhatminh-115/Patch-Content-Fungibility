## 1. Main manuscript

The main paper uses **8 figures + 3 data tables**. Submission-facing figures are generated by `scripts/build_paper_figures_v4.py` and stored under `figures/paper_final_v4/`.

### Figure 1 — Conceptual overview
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure1_overview.svg`  
**Role:** Define the activation-substitution estimand and summarize the three boundaries of the result: late content tolerance, geometry/diversity constraints, and replaceability versus compression. The schematic explicitly keeps upstream image processing, [CLS], token slots, sequence length, model weights, and downstream blocks fixed.

### Figure 2 — Depth emergence
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure2_depthwise.svg`  
**Role:** Show the 25% replacement intervention across DeiT-Tiny, DeiT-Small, ViT-B/16 AugReg, and DINOv2 ViT-S/14. The claim is a depth-dependent transition into a late replacement-tolerant regime; no universal block index is claimed.

### Figure 3 — Dense fraction dose-response
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure3_geometry_diversity.svg`  
**Role:** Show geometry controls at 50% replacement, grouped token-position diversity, and PC1 versus an energy-matched random 1D direction under complete replacement.

### Figure 4 — Geometry constraint
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure4_anisotropic_geometry.svg`  
**Role:** Compare the aligned calibration centroid against coordinate permutation, sign inversion, and zero replacement. The conclusion is feature-space compatibility with learned coordinates/orientation, not full-manifold recovery.

### Figure 5 — Diversity constraint
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure5_value_path_cancellation.svg`  
**Role:** Compare full-block and frozen-attention Value-path transmission across coherent, random-sign, and checkerboard perturbations in four architectures.

### Figure 6 — Joint token–feature response geometry
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure6_joint_stream_geometry.svg`  
**Role:** Show mean final-logit response across eight token-space patterns and five feature-space directions for DeiT-Small at Block 8 and ViT-B/16 AugReg at Block 7. The same N=100-image cohort estimates directions and scores outcomes, so this is descriptive evidence rather than held-out validation or an inferential interaction test.

### Figure 7 — End-to-end functional transmission
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure7_end_to_end_operator.svg`  
**Role:** Compare single-block and end-to-end damage-prediction associations and multi-block top-mode versus near-null-mode damage ratios across four architectures.

### Figure 8 — Operator-aware compression
**Path:** `figures/paper_final_v4/figure8_operator_compression.svg`  
**Role:** Present confirmatory accuracy-versus-token curves for four architectures, with Table II listing rank-16 and rank-32 results at each model's most aggressive tested budget.

### Table 1 — Cross-architecture synthesis
**Role:** Summarize architecture, training regime, readout topology, clean performance, primary depth, centroid recovery, geometry effect, diversity effect, and learned-1D evidence for all four models.
