## Required Wording

- C2a activation-control Top-1 is N=1,000 per DeiT architecture; C2b's +1.474 PCA-basis result is N=100 operator-space evidence, not classification evidence.
- Section 5.2 primary and reduced attention outcomes: N=100 images/model; the direction is estimated from the first 4 images, and Tiny/DINO are reduced replications only.
- Section 5.1 functional geometry: N=100 first calibration images/model at depths 5, 7, 8, and 10 for all four architectures; threshold/effective-rank trends are exploratory, and the DINOv2 near-null fraction is cutoff-sensitive.
- Calibration-derived PC1 comparison: N=1,000 evaluation images and disjoint N=1,000 calibration images per architecture at complete replacement; DeiT uses five seeds and ViT-B/DINOv2 use three. DINOv2 Top-1 remains near floor; its margin is a separate outcome. No rank-1 sufficiency claim.
- Joint-stream geometry: N=100 images per model, separate from the functional-geometry image pilot and the multi-block N=100 perturbation study.
- Section 5.3 operator prediction: N=100 perturbation vectors/model, 25 per family, evaluated on a fixed 20-image reference batch; vectors are the correlation unit. Do not call these N=100 images.
- C6–C8 strict confirmatory: N=1,000 held-out images per architecture.
- C9b/C12b/C14 real-final classification: N=1,000 held-out images per architecture.
- Never combine counts from distinct experiments into one sample-size claim.
